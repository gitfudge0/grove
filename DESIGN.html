Assumptions

  • Palette was visually inferred and regularized around approved Smoked plum values.
  • Dark received scrutiny; light was derived.
  • IBM Plex Sans and Blex Mono Nerd Font Mono are bundled product fonts.
  • Spacing and radius are tidy regularizations. Motion, z, opacity, blur, and shadow depth are defaults from static screenshots.
  • Reduced motion collapses duration to 0ms and skips transforms.
  • Dark surface depth comes from color and seams, not visible shadow.
