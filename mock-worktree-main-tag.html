Why not keep main visible during hover?

The alternative — letting the tag sit permanently at the far right and having the hover icons appear to its left — means two variable-width things compete for the same row on hover. The label has to eat the difference, so a main-worktree row ellipsizes more aggressively than its siblings the moment you hover it, and the tag crowds right up against the trailing icon:

grove main
Cramped, and the row's effective label width now depends on hover state in a way none of the other rows do.
